# FCC macro-site classification used in the final map

The external map uses the FCC site-type vocabulary rather than project-specific categories:

- **Macro_Tower** — outdoor macrocell mounted on a monopole, guyed tower, or freestanding tower.
- **Macro_Building** — outdoor macrocell mounted on a building.
- **Macro_Other** — outdoor macrocell on another support type, such as a silo or water tower.

Official definition: https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-408453A2.pdf

Applied to this corridor:

- Macro_Tower: 10 sites.
- Macro_Building: 7 sites.
- Macro_Other: 1 site.

A black outline ring means a local street/site image is retained in the evidence package. It does not mean that the carrier identity or live radio status was visually established.
